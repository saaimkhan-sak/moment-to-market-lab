# Project charter

**Question.** Which recurring, objectively detected hockey moments produce the strongest and most durable independently observable public-attention response?

**Users.** Content, marketing, partnerships, communications, and strategy teams.

**Public-data boundary.** Outputs describe public information seeking, earned-media volume/context, official public video metadata, and market context. They do not establish fan identity, purchase intent, attendance, ticket revenue, sell-through, sponsor value, partnership inventory, renewal likelihood, causal conversion, or internal workflow behavior.

**Historical release.** 2015–16 through 2025–26; all 32 current clubs; an unbalanced club-season panel in which Vegas enters in 2017–18, Seattle in 2021–22, and Utah in 2024–25; explicit Arizona/Utah identity validity; one shared codebase and route-level club views.

**Independent outcomes.** Wikimedia daily pageviews and exact-name, precision-audited GDELT normalized earned-media volume are modeled separately. “Stable” requires both channels to have at least ten modeled and ten isolated observations, agree in modeled and club-local raw direction, and have modeled and raw 95% intervals excluding zero. YouTube remains descriptive unless repeated project snapshots exist.
